About Me

Hello! My name is Hlulani Tyrone Mthombeni, and I am a passionate Information Technology student specializing in Software Development at Rosebank International University in Johannesburg, Gauteng, South Africa. I am currently in my third year of study and have developed a strong foundation in software development, web technologies, database systems, information security, and systems analysis. My educational journey began at Singobuli Primary School before continuing my secondary education at Maroleni Secondary School, where I successfully completed my National Senior Certificate (Matric) in 2023. In 2025, I enrolled at Rosebank International University to pursue my passion for technology and software development. Since then, I have worked on numerous projects involving programming, artificial intelligence, chatbot development, web applications, desktop applications, and database-driven systems. My goal is to become a highly skilled Software Developer capable of designing innovative digital solutions that solve real-world problems and improve the lives of users through technology.
