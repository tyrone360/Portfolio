Project Overview

Understanding the purpose, objectives and development of the Haute Couture e-commerce platform.

About the Project

Haute Couture is a fashion e-commerce website developed to provide customers with a convenient online shopping experience. The platform allows customers to explore fashion products, add items to their shopping cart, review their orders and submit checkout information.

The system was developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with LocalStorage supporting cart management and EmailJS handling customer order notifications.

Problem Statement

Traditional shopping experiences can require customers to physically visit stores to browse available fashion products and place orders. This project addresses the need for a convenient digital shopping platform where customers can explore products and submit orders online.
